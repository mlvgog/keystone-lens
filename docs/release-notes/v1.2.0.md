**Keystone Lens now has a game addon: the top players' pick rates, builds and gear, inside World of Warcraft.**

## Download

- **`KeystoneLens-Setup-1.2.0.exe`:** the installer (recommended). Already on 1.1.0? The app updates itself.
- **`KeystoneLens-Portable-1.2.0.exe`:** a single exe, no install.
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`:** checksums to verify the download.

The exe is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new

### The Keystone Lens addon
- **On your talent window:**
  - the pick rate of the top players on every talent, with choice splits;
  - a red frame on popular talents you skip, an orange frame on rare ones you take;
  - a dungeon switch that starts on the dungeon of your keystone.
- **`/kl`:**
  - the popular builds per dungeon, which one is closest to yours, and their import strings;
  - the most used gear, enchants and gems per slot, with yours marked, and their secondary stats next to yours.
- **Item tooltips:** how many top players of your spec use an item.

### In the app
- **⚙ Settings → Game addon:**
  - finds your World of Warcraft folder;
  - installs or updates the addon;
  - lists the specs the addon has. Every Mythic+ spec you look at is sent to the addon, and your characters' specs are refreshed in the background once a day.
- **Compare with my character:** characters the addon saved appear under "From the game addon". No SimulationCraft paste needed.
- **Long dialogs scroll** on small screens.

The addon only gets Mythic+ data. Warcraft Logs data (damage, raid) stays in the app. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/mlvgog/keystone-lens/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) for what is written to the game folder.
