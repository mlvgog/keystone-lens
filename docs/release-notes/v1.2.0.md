**See which races the top players pick, with their racial abilities, in the app and in game. The Warcraft Logs features are now optional.**

## Download

- **`KeystoneLens-Setup-1.2.0.exe`:** the installer (recommended). Installs for your Windows user only, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an uninstaller.
- **`KeystoneLens-Portable-1.2.0.exe`:** a single exe, no install.
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`:** checksums to verify the download.

**Coming from 1.1.0?** You don't need to download anything. The installed app finds this version by itself, downloads it in the background, and shows **Restart to update** in the title bar. The portable exe says a new version is out and opens this page. Coming from 1.0.0, install this version by hand once.

The exe is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new

### Popular races
- **A Races card in the Gear overview** shows how many of the top players play each race. Alliance races are blue, Horde races red, and races that can be either are neutral. Your own race is marked when you compare with your character. The dungeon filter applies to it.
- **Click a race for its racial abilities,** for the spec's class. Blood Elves only show your class's Arcane Torrent, and Dracthyr show Soar only for Evokers. Hover one for its Wowhead tooltip.
- **The Players tab has a Race column.**

### Warcraft Logs is now optional
- **Off by default.** The Damage tab and Raid mode need your own Warcraft Logs API key, so they no longer show up until you want them. While they are off, the app never contacts Warcraft Logs.
- **Turn them on in ⚙ Settings → Warcraft Logs.** A short explanation comes first: what you get, why Warcraft Logs needs your own key, and how to make one in two minutes. The fields for your key appear after that.
- **Already set up?** If you had a Warcraft Logs key saved, both stay on, as before.

### Game addon 1.1.0
- **Popular races** at the bottom of the `/kl` window. Each race shows its portrait and %, and your race is ticked. Click one for its racial abilities, with the game's own names and tooltips.
- The app updates the addon copy it installed. Type `/reload` in game afterwards. Races appear once the app has written new data: look at your spec again, or wait for the daily refresh.

Each player's race comes from raider.io. The racial abilities come from the game's data, through SimulationCraft. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/mlvgog/keystone-lens/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) for what is sent where.
