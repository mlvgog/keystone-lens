**Keystone Lens now updates itself and comes with a game addon: the top players' pick rates, builds and gear, inside World of Warcraft.**

## Download

- **`KeystoneLens-Setup-1.1.0.exe`:** the installer (recommended). Installs for your Windows user only, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an uninstaller.
- **`KeystoneLens-Portable-1.1.0.exe`:** a single exe, no install.
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`:** checksums to verify the download.

Coming from 1.0.0? Install this version by hand once: 1.0.0 can't update itself. Your settings, saved characters and cache are kept.

The exe is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new

### Automatic updates
- **The installed app updates itself.** It checks for a new version when it starts and every 6 hours. It downloads the update in the background and installs it when you restart or close the app. A green **Restart to update** button appears in the title bar when one is ready.
- **Your choice.** ⚙ Settings → **Updates**:
  - **Automatic** (default): as above.
  - **Only tell me**: shows the new version and installs it when you click.
  - **Off**: never checks on its own. "Check now" still works.
- **Portable exe.** It can't replace itself, so it tells you when a new version is out and opens its download page.

### The Keystone Lens game addon
- **On your talent window:**
  - the pick rate of the top players on every talent, with choice splits;
  - a red frame on popular talents you skip, an orange frame on rare ones you take;
  - a dungeon switch that starts on the dungeon of your keystone.
- **`/kl`:**
  - the top players' gear as a character pane, with the most used item and its % in every slot. Click a slot for all options, enchants and gems, with yours marked;
  - bars with their secondary stats next to yours, as rating and %;
  - a side pane with the popular builds per dungeon and the talents each one changes in yours. Each build has a Copy button for its string, and **Show on talents** frames those changes on your talent window.
- **Item tooltips:** how many top players of your spec use an item.

### In the app
- **⚙ Settings → Game addon:**
  - finds your World of Warcraft folder;
  - installs the addon, and keeps the copy it installed up to date whenever the app comes with a newer build (`/reload` in game afterwards);
  - lists the specs the addon has. Every Mythic+ spec you look at is sent to the addon, and your characters' specs are refreshed in the background once a day.
- **A notice when the addon isn't installed or is outdated:** a banner with Install and Not now, and a dot on ⚙.
- **Compare with my character:** characters the addon saved appear under "From the game addon". No SimulationCraft paste needed.
- **Long dialogs scroll** on small screens.

The addon only gets Mythic+ data; Warcraft Logs data (damage, raid) stays in the app. Update checks only ask GitHub for this project's newest release. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/mlvgog/keystone-lens/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) for what is sent and what is written to the game folder.
