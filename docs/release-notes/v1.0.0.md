The first release of **Keystone Lens**: see what the best players of your spec run in Mythic+ and in raid, and how your character compares.

## Download

- **`KeystoneLens-Setup-1.0.0.exe`:** the installer (recommended). Installs for your Windows user only, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an uninstaller.
- **`KeystoneLens-Portable-1.0.0.exe`:** a single exe, no install.
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`:** checksums to verify the download.

The exe is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's in it

### Mythic+
- **Talents:** pick rates of the top players of any spec on an in-game-style talent tree, plus popular builds with copyable import strings, and filters for dungeon and hero tree.
- **Gear:** item usage per slot with exact stats, enchants, gems, embellishments, tier pieces and where each item drops, with Wowhead tooltips on hover.
- **Players:** the players and the keys their data comes from. Click a player to inspect them.
- **Damage:** median DPS/HPS per dungeon and key level from Warcraft Logs, and your own logged runs against them (optional, needs your free Warcraft Logs API client).

### Raid (new)
- **Per boss and difficulty:** the top 100 logged kills of your spec on every boss of the current raid, with each player's talents and gear at that kill (Warcraft Logs).
- **Picked differently on a boss:** select a boss to see which talents the top players change for it.

### Compare with my character
- **Two ways to load:** look your character up on raider.io, or paste your **SimulationCraft** `/simc` export. The export has your exact talents, saved loadouts, crafted stats and catalysed items.
- **What you see:**
  - your talents on the tree;
  - how popular each of your items is;
  - your secondary stat ratings against the top players' average.

## Data

Players and keys come from raider.io's public API. Tooltips come from Wowhead's official tooltip script. Talent trees and item stats are built from SimulationCraft and wago.tools game data. Damage and raid data come from Warcraft Logs, with your own API client.

Keystone Lens has no accounts, telemetry or server. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/mlvgog/keystone-lens/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
