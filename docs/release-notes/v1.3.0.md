**New: the flasks, food and augment runes the top players use, in the app and in game. And the app and the CurseForge addon are now in step.**

## Download

- **`KeystoneLens-Setup-1.3.0.exe`:** the installer (recommended). Installs for your Windows user only, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts and an uninstaller.
- **`KeystoneLens-Portable-1.3.0.exe`:** a single exe, no install.
- **`SHA256SUMS.txt`:** checksums to verify the download.

**Coming from 1.1.0 or newer?** The installed app updates itself and shows **Restart to update** in the title bar. The portable exe opens this page.

The exe is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## New

- **Consumables.** The Gear tab's overview has a new **Consumables** card: the flasks, food and augment runes the top players use, with each one's share, overall or per dungeon. Food is named after the stat it raises (for example "Haste food"). Hover one for its tooltip. The data comes from the buffs Raider.IO records at each boss pull of runs logged with the Raider.IO client, so it covers the players whose runs were logged (most of the top players); the card says how many. Potions and weapon oils are not recorded there. The first time you look at each spec after updating, the app downloads the top players' keys again (once) to get their consumables, so that first look takes a little longer.
- **Weekly data for every spec, also through the app.** The game addon on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/keystone-lens) comes with data for every spec (the top 50 players world-wide, every dungeon), renewed every Wednesday. If you installed the addon with the app, the app now adds the same weekly data and keeps it current. Specs you look at in the app still get fresher data on top: per spec, the addon shows whichever is newer. You can turn it off in **⚙ Settings → Game addon**.
- **App and CurseForge side by side.** If you also use CurseForge, the app leaves CurseForge's copy of the addon and its weekly data alone and only writes its own data. Settings says where the weekly data comes from.
- **The same addon version everywhere.** Each app release now brings its game addon to CurseForge too, so the app and CurseForge always carry the same addon version.

## Game addon 1.3.0

- **Consumables in `/kl`:** under the secondary stats, the flask, food and augment rune the top players use most, with their share. Hover one for the other options and whether you carry it; a tick shows when the top flask or rune is in your bags.
- Ready for patch 12.1.7: it won't show as out of date when the patch goes live.

The app replaces the addon copy it installed; type `/reload` in game afterwards. If you installed the addon from CurseForge, CurseForge brings 1.3.0 at the same time, with the consumables of every spec in its weekly data.
